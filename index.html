Hello, I’m Deborah.

I’m an aspiring customer support professional with an interest in helping fintech and B2B SaaS businesses create better customer experiences.

I understand that behind every support request is a customer who needs an answer, a solution or simply someone willing to listen. My goal is simple: to make every customer interaction helpful, professional and reassuring.

I’m especially interested in email and live chat support, where I can help customers navigate technical difficulties, resolve billing concerns, understand product features and get the most from the services they use.

Understand the problem

I ask the right questions first, so I solve the real issue rather than the assumed one.

Communicate clearly

Warm, concise and jargon-free replies that reassure customers and set clear next steps.

Work towards resolution

I own the ticket, follow up on pending issues and escalate complex problems with full context.
